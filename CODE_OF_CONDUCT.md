# Code of conduct

We want OpenSeek to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone who takes part in it.

## Expected behaviour

* Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not people.
* Give and accept constructive feedback.
* Keep discussion on topic and technical.
* Respect privacy: do not post other people's personal data, tokens, invitation codes or private media links.

## Unacceptable behaviour

* Harassment, insults, threats, discrimination or demeaning language.
* Sexualised content or unwelcome attention.
* Publishing someone's private information without permission.
* Sustained disruption of discussions.
* Requests for help obtaining or sharing media without the right to do so (see `docs/RESPONSIBLE-USE.md`); such requests will be closed.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and contributions that violate this code, and may ban people from the project spaces.
To report a conduct problem, contact the maintainer through the repository's private channels. A contact address is to be added here by the maintainer.

This code of conduct applies in project spaces and when representing the project in public. It is inspired by the Contributor Covenant.
