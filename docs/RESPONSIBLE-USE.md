# Responsible use

## What OpenSeek is

OpenSeek is free, self-hosted software. It generates small preview images and a WebVTT index from media your own server can read, so that a video player can show
scene thumbnails while you scrub.

## What the maintainer does and does not do

The maintainer:

* does not operate other people's servers;
* does not host thumbnail libraries or any media;
* provides no movie or video downloads, links or indexes;
* runs no central content-sharing service, no accounts and no telemetry.

Every installation is independent. Each server owner controls their own installation and decides which devices are authorised, whether sharing is on,
whether guests may contribute, and what media the server reads.

## What you are responsible for

You must hold the rights, permissions or applicable legal exceptions needed to generate and share thumbnails from the media you point OpenSeek at, and to give
others access to them. Thumbnails are derived from the video itself. Whether creating or sharing them is permitted depends on your jurisdiction, on the licence or
terms attached to the media and on the services you use to obtain it. If you invite guests or enable Contributor Mode, you are responsible for whom you authorise and for what
they can reach through your server.

Contributor Mode and the library are tools for people who already have access to the same media. Do not use them to circumvent the access controls, terms of service
or paywalls of a service.

## Limits of this notice

This notice does not remove anyone's legal responsibility and is not legal advice. Nothing in this repository claims that any particular use is lawful. If in doubt, ask a
qualified lawyer in your jurisdiction.

Technical privacy features (TLS, redacted logs, no persisted links) protect against eavesdropping and accidental disclosure. They do not make an activity lawful and do not hide
it from the services whose media you read.
